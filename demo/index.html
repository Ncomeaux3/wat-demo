WAT
Workflows · Agents · Tools

Ask an AI to handle a whole job in one shot and it will sometimes make things up. And errors stack: if each step of a process is right 9 times out of 10, a five-step chain is right only about 6 times out of 10. WAT splits the work instead: AI handles the judgment calls (sorting, choosing, wording), ordinary software handles the facts (looking up records, applying policy — exact, same answer every time), and a person approves anything before a customer sees it.

AI — reads & decides (judgment) Software — exact rules, same answer every time Human — the final say
